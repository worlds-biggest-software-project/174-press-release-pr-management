# Press Release & PR Management

> Part of the [worlds-biggest-software-project](https://github.com/worlds-biggest-software-project) initiative.
>
> An AI-native, open-source PR platform combining journalist targeting, distribution, coverage tracking, and analytics for communications teams of every size.

Press Release & PR Management is a candidate open-source project that brings together press release distribution, a journalist database, coverage tracking, and PR analytics in a single platform. It is intended for corporate communications teams, PR agencies, investor relations officers, and growth-stage marketing teams who need modern tooling without enterprise-tier pricing.

---

## Why Press Release & PR Management?

- Enterprise incumbents (Cision, Muck Rack) charge $10,000–$50,000+ per year and are reported to have complex UIs and legacy architectures.
- Pricing transparency is a recurring complaint across Cision, Meltwater, and Muck Rack — most platforms require custom enterprise quoting.
- Mid-market tools (Prezly, Prowly) are more affordable but lack comprehensive media monitoring, wire integration, or full journalist databases. Prowly is being phased out by Semrush as of December 2025.
- Wire distribution is commoditising while AI-powered pitch targeting is displacing mass distribution; 69% of PR professionals already report using generative AI (Muck Rack 2025 State of PR Report).
- No incumbent platform quantifies journalist relationship health, predicts placement likelihood before send, or matches releases to outlet-specific style guides — clear gaps for an AI-native challenger.

## Key Features

### Journalist Database & CRM

- Journalist/media contact database targeting 500K+ contacts with beat and job-change tracking.
- Tagging, segmentation, and media list building from database search.
- Pitch history, contact assignment, and shared internal notes per contact.
- Email tracking with open, click, reply, and bounce monitoring.

### Press Release Authoring & Owned Media

- Drag-and-drop or rich text editor with multimedia support (photos, video, social embeds).
- Branded, SEO-optimised newsroom with multi-language support and social feed integration.
- Template library and outlet-specific style guide matching for outlet-voice generation.
- Embed-in-email release support so full content is visible without click-through.

### Distribution & Outreach

- Email pitching with personalised campaigns, follow-up sequences, and engagement tracking.
- Integration paths for wire networks (PR Newswire, Business Wire) and social distribution.
- GDPR-, CAN-SPAM-, and CASL-aware contact handling for journalist outreach.
- Optional regulatory filing integration (SEC EDGAR, SEDAR) for investor relations workflows.

### Monitoring & Analytics

- Real-time media monitoring across news, social, podcasts, and broadcast.
- Sentiment analysis, share-of-voice, and entity-level scoring (journalist, competitor, spokesperson).
- Coverage attribution that correlates pitches to resulting press coverage.
- Dashboards for reach, opens, clicks, and campaign ROI.

### AI-Powered Capabilities

- AI journalist targeting based on beat history and past coverage analysis.
- Relationship health scoring derived from open rates, response patterns, and coverage outcomes.
- Predictive send-time optimisation modelling news cycles and journalist availability.
- Placement prediction scoring before send and AI-assisted release drafting in outlet voice.

## AI-Native Advantage

Incumbent platforms treat AI as a bolt-on feature; this project is designed AI-first. Automated journalist targeting using real-time beat and coverage history can replace manual database searching and improve pitch relevance. AI-generated drafts trained on outlet-specific style guides reduce agency writing burden, while predictive analytics on timing optimise embargo and send scheduling. Dynamic relationship scoring surfaces journalist relationship health in a way no current CRM provides.

---

## Tech Stack & Deployment

The platform is expected to be deployable in self-hosted and managed cloud modes, with GDPR-compliant journalist contact handling. It targets alignment with established standards including Schema.org NewsArticle markup for SEO, IPTC News Standards for metadata, and the PESO model for integrated PR measurement. Distribution integrations target PR Newswire and Business Wire syndication protocols, and regulatory workflows align with SEC EDGAR and SEDAR filing systems.

## Market Context

The PR software market was valued at approximately $1.5B in 2024 and is projected to reach $3.3B by 2033 at a 9.5% CAGR (Future Market Report, 2025). Enterprise platforms charge $10,000–$50,000+ annually, mid-market tools sit at $1,000–$3,000/yr, and per-release wire pricing ranges from $349 to $2,000+. Primary buyers are corporate communications directors, PR agency account managers, investor relations officers, marketing managers at growth-stage companies, and government/NGO communications teams.
